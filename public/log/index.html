The Log.

What happened at the bench this week, written by the person it happened to. Published Thursdays, including the weeks when the honest entry is that nothing was built.

How this differs from the Essays

The essays are numbered, edited, and independent of the company that funds this site. The log is not. It is first person, it is about ZKNOT, Inc.'s own work, and it is written by its founder. We keep the two apart so that the independence claimed for one is not quietly spent on the other.

It is here because a company that publishes what breaks should publish it where anyone can read it, not only where its customers are.
